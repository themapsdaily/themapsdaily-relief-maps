# Chhattisgarh hypsometric palette
#
# NOTE ON FORMAT: relief.py accepts a stop only if the WHOLE line is a bare
# #RRGGBB (re.fullmatch), so annotations must live on their own comment lines --
# an inline "#A1B06D  # 51 m" is silently dropped and the state falls back to the
# default palette. Keep the hex lines bare.
#
# Built against the MEASURED hypsometry, not by eye: ~77% of the state sits
# between 200 and 700 m (modal band 300-400 m) and only 2% is above 800 m. These
# stops are read through BL_COLORGAMMA=0.8, which spreads s1..s5 over 1.2%..85%
# of the surface instead of stacking four colours onto the top 2.6%.
#
# Ecology, low -> high: the Sabari/Indravati lowland and the Chhattisgarh rice
# bowl read as warm paddy yellow-green; the sal forests of the Bastar plateau and
# Jashpur deepen to a dark forest trough over Abujhmarh and the Maikal range;
# laterite is then exposed on the plateau edges (olive), warming through the
# Mainpat/Pendra ochre to a warm TAN -- not snow-white -- on Gaurlata and
# Bailadila, which top out at ~1265 m in the tropics.
#
# Greens stay in the series' yellow-green family (hue 93-108, sat 0.28-0.31), in
# line with Jharkhand / Manipur / Kerala.
#
