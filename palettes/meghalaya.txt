# Meghalaya hypsometric palette — "the abode of clouds", the wettest state on
# earth (Mawsynram / Sohra). Built against the MEASURED z11 hypsometry, which is
# unusually EVEN for this series (12.5% under 100 m, ~5% per 100 m band through
# the middle, 4.9% above 1600 m) — so evenly spaced stops all earn their pixels
# and no BL_COLORGAMMA correction is needed. Stop elevations (12 m floor,
# 1964 m max) are noted per line.
#
# The arc is a LUSHNESS arc, not a simple green->rock ramp:
#   - the border lowlands (Bangladesh plain south, Brahmaputra fringe north) are
#     bright wet paddy green;
#   - it DARKENS into the 700-1100 m belt, which catches the monsoon head-on —
#     tropical evergreen forest and the Khasi sacred groves. This is the darkest,
#     richest green in the series, and it is the point of the map;
#   - above that the Khasi/Jaintia plateau surface opens into Khasi pine and
#     rolling GRASSLAND, so it LIGHTENS back through olive to tawny gold at
#     Shillong Peak — real high-plateau grass, not rock and not snow.
#     (Same treatment as Kerala's Munnar shola-grassland high ranges.)
#
# Stays in the series YELLOW-green family (greens hue ~88-110, sat 0.27-0.34) —
# an emerald/blue-green drift reads as "artificial" next to the other states.
# NO snow (BL_SNOWLINE=1.1): the 1965 m high point is grass, not ice.
# One #RRGGBB per line, low elevation -> high elevation.
