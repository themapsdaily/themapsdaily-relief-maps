# Arunachal Pradesh hypsometric palette — the wettest, most densely forested
# state in the Himalaya (~79% forest cover, the highest share of any large
# Indian state). Its elevation range (107-6996 m after DEM_CLAMP_LO) is almost
# identical to Jammu & Kashmir's (242-7071 m), so J&K's ramp maps ~1:1 by
# elevation — but the ECOLOGY is the opposite. J&K turns barren stone early
# because the Pir Panjal throws a rain shadow over Zanskar/Ladakh; Arunachal
# takes the full force of the Bay of Bengal monsoon, so forest climbs to
# ~3,500-4,000 m and there is no cold desert at all. This ramp therefore holds
# GREEN much longer and much DEEPER than J&K's, and only turns to stone in the
# top three stops (which sit under snow anyway).
#
# The 10 stops land at roughly:
#    107 m  Brahmaputra alluvial fringe — Namsai, Pasighat, the Assam border
#    872 m  subtropical broadleaf / bamboo foothills (Itanagar 640, Khonsa 800)
#   1638 m  dense tropical + subtropical evergreen — the signature forest belt
#   2403 m  temperate broadleaf, oak and rhododendron (the darkest trough)
#   3169 m  upper temperate conifer, hemlock and fir (Tawang 3048)
#   3934 m  treeline, dwarf rhododendron and alpine scrub
#   4700 m  alpine meadow and grass above the treeline (~BL_SNOWLINE)
#   5465 m  barren rock and scree
#   6231 m  summit scree (mostly under permanent snow)
#   6996 m  near-white (Kangto massif; true summit 7,060 m)
#
# The ramp ROTATES HUE continuously (95 -> 105 -> 98 -> 82 -> 62 -> 45 -> 33)
# the way Himachal's and J&K's do. A first draft held hue ~100 flat across the
# five green stops; because ~80% of Arunachal lies below 3,300 m that painted
# the whole state one shade (100% of terrain green, hue p10-p90 only 86-111,
# against Himachal's 32-117) and it read as a flat slab. Holding green through
# s3 (~2,400 m) but turning olive at s4 gives the alpine zone its own tone.
# Snow (white) is blended on top above BL_SNOWLINE. Greens sit at hue 95-105,
# inside the series' yellow-green family (Manipur/Goa/Jharkhand/Kerala ~95-108);
# an emerald hue ~138 reads "artificial" next to the other states. Filmic and
# desaturated, not neon.
# One #RRGGBB per line, low elevation -> high elevation.
