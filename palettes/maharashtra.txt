# Maharashtra hypsometric palette — a big PLATEAU state with a coast, so its
# hypsometry is unlike any state done so far: ~80% of the surface sits between
# 200 and 700 m (the Deccan basalt plateau) and only ~0.4% is above 1000 m (the
# Sahyadri crestline). Stops are therefore tuned to the measured DEM histogram,
# not spread evenly by eye — otherwise the top three stops paint 0.2% of the map
# and the plateau reads as one flat green mass.
#
# Range after despiking is ~0-1539 m (z9), so the 10 stops land at ~171 m each:
#     0 m  Konkan coastal plain + the Wainganga/Wardha valleys of E Vidarbha
#   171 m  Konkan midland, teak lowland                      ] the wet, green
#   342 m  forested eastern Vidarbha (Gadchiroli/Chandrapur) ] DARK TROUGH
#   513 m  plateau edge
#   684 m  the modal Deccan plateau — semi-arid Desh/Marathwada rain shadow,
#          so this band turns OLIVE rather than continuing green
#   855 m  upper plateau / Ghat shoulder
#  1026 m  Ghat crest
#  1197 m  high Sahyadri basalt
#  1368 m  summit tablelands (Mahabaleshwar-type grass plateaus)
#  1539 m  the highest crests (Kalsubai 1646 m, Salher 1567 m — both truncated
#          to ~1500 m by the z9 grid, which is why the top stop is reached)
#
# Greens stay in the SERIES family (hue 88-100, sat 0.25-0.30) — same yellow-green
# as Manipur/Goa/Jharkhand/Kerala, NOT an emerald. Warm stops are basalt ochre
# and dry-season grass gold, never rock-grey and never snow (BL_SNOWLINE=1.1).
# One #RRGGBB per line, low elevation -> high elevation.
