# Assam hypsometric palette — the Brahmaputra state. ~80% of Assam is the vast,
# near-flat Brahmaputra valley (and the smaller Barak valley in the south) at
# ~40-90 m: wet paddy, tea gardens and wetland — so the DOMINANT colour is a
# bright, wet lowland green, brighter than the Imphal-valley base of Manipur
# (which sits at ~780 m). From that valley floor the land climbs only on the
# fringes — the Karbi Anglong plateau and the North Cachar / Barail hills in the
# south-centre — through dense subtropical evergreen forest (the darkest, richest
# green, ~600-1200 m), then olive and khaki on the upper hills, to tan/gold on
# the highest Barail ridges (~1960 m). There is NO snow (BL_SNOWLINE=1.1): the
# high point is forest-and-grass, not ice.
#
# Held in the series' YELLOW-green family (greens hue ~90-110, sat ~0.28-0.34) so
# it sits beside Manipur, Meghalaya, Kerala and Goa; an emerald/blue-green drift
# reads as "artificial". Because the state is so bottom-heavy, tune the spread
# with BL_COLORGAMMA against the MEASURED hypsometry rather than eyeballing.
